                                               Internal clock, manual reset
The new firmware adds an internal clock, its speed can be tapped by holding the left transpose button and clicking the encoder knob.
In live mode you may want to avoid this action, since it will toggle the state of transposition. To reset arpeggiator and sequencer hold
the right transpose button and click the encoder.

KEYBOARD AND ARPEGGIATOR SETTINGS




              - 5 -
Arp state, hold
LT disable/enable arpeggiator
RT disable/enable hold, when enabled the arpeggiator
will continue play the arpeggiated sequence even when note
plates are not touched


Arp direction
LT direction of arpeggiation:
- up
- down
- up-down
- random

Arp variation
LT status and behavior:
- off
- add variation after the arpeggiated sequence ends
- add variation after each arpeggiated note
BT distance of added notes (1 to 12 semitones)
RT number of variations (1 to 5 times)



              - 6 -
Arp clock
LT individual clock rate of arpeggiator, which can be the
division or multiplication of the internal clock/external sync
(:32, :24, :16, :12, :8, :6, :4, :3, :2, *1, *2,
*3, *4, *6, *8)


Arp rhythm
Applies a rhythmic pattern to the arpeggiators clock source.
LT length of pattern (2-16)
BT select step to edit
RT turn selected step off-on




               - 7 -
Behaviour of transpose plates
LT behaviour:
- latching (takes effect as soon a note plate is touched)
- latching immediate (takes effect immediately)
- momentary (takes effect till it's touched)
- octave (from -3 to +3 octave)

Pressure transpose
When enabled the outgoing note gets transposed as pressure
is applied to note plates. This feature is most usefull when
both the arpeggiator and sequencer are disabled. The basic
idea comes from Skot Wiedmann's Hyve synthesizer.
LT status and number of transpositions:
- x- [off]
- x2
- x4
- x8
RT distance of transposition (1 to 12 semitones)

NOTE SEQUENCER SETTINGS




         - 9 -
Sequencer state
LT disable/enable sequencer




Sequence recorder
LT press to disable/enable recording
RT mode of recording:
- new sequence: when recording is enabled the notes of
the sequence are entered by playing the note plates, to skip
a step forward press the upper transpose plate or rotate the
encoder CW, to undo a step (move back) rotate it CCW
- overdub: when recording is enabled the sequencer
keeps playing, to overwrite the programmed melody just
hit the note plates (ratcheting gate variations are pressure
sensitive), press upper transpose plate to mute gates per step




              - 10 -
Edit gate/note per step
LT select step (1 to sequence length)
BT set note per step
RT set gate type of selected step:
- ------------ (off)
- IIIIIIIIIIII (full on)
- IIIIII------
- III---III---
- II--II--II--
- I--I--I--I--

Sequence length
LT number of steps in the sequence (2 to 32)




Sequencer direction
LT direction of sequencing:
- forward
- backward
- ping-pong
- random


             - 11 -
Note variation
LT probability, that a random note will be added to the
sequence (0% to 99%)
RT scope of random notes, the maximum distance of random
notes compared to the pre-programmed notes of sequence
(2 to 32 semitones)


Sequencer clock
LT individual clock rate of sequencer, which can be the
division or multiplication of the internal clock/external sync
(:32, :24, :16, :12, :8, :6, :4, :3, :2, *1, *2,
*3, *4)




              - 12 -
Sequencer reset
LT disable/enable reset from note plates (when enabled
the sequencer will restart [reset its playhead] when a new
note is played on the note plates)



Gate pressure
LT disable/enable pressure control of active gates (when
enabled pressure applied to note plates will influence the pre-
programmed gate types of steps)



CV mode
LT behavior of sequencer note CV output:
- continuous: note output will change according to the
programmed note values per step
- gated: note output will be updated only when a step has
non-off gate type

PRESSURE OUTPUT SETTINGS




          - 14 -
Pressure output
LT available sources:
- pressure: voltage on the pressure output will
continuously follow the pressure applied to note plates
- velocity: voltage on pressure output will represent the
force with which a note plate is touched (it takes some
time to measure the velocity, therefore it's delayed by a few
milliseconds compared to the event)
- AD: attack/decay envelope generator
- AR: attack/sustain/release envelope generator
- LO: looped AD envelope generator
- recorder: tape or sequencer output
- random: outputs a random voltage level, runs from the
same trigger source as the envelope generator
- poly1: two note polyphony, the pressure output acts as
the note cv output of second note
- poly2: same as above, but with a different behavior of the
polyphony algorithm (each new note appears on the main
note output, the previous note will moved to the pressure
output)




             - 15 -
Pressure recorder
Press a note plate to record pressure information.
RT mode of recording:
- new sequence/tape: pressing a note plate will restart the
tape/sequence, the duration of contact determines the length
of tape (the length of sequence is set by sequence lenth
parameter, see below)
- overdub: pressing a plate will momentarily enable
recording, overwriting the previously recorded data on tape
or value of current step of sequencer

BT vernier of duration (0 to 3)
RT shape of segment (exp, lin, log)


Decay/release duration, shape
LT coarse adjustment of duration (0 to 63)
BT vernier of duration (0 to 3)
RT shape of segment (exp, lin, log)


Modulation of attack duration
LT amount of modulation (0 to 63)

Modulation of dec./rel. duration

            - 17 -
Modulation of envelope level
LT amount of modulation (0 to 63)
BT mode of modulation
- T: increasing modulation attenuates the peak (top) of
envelope
- B: increasing modulation shifts the fundament (bottom)
of envelope
RT source of modulation (pressure, velocity,
recorder, random)

Envelope gate source, hard reset
LT source of gate sygnal that controls envelope generator:
- automatic
- note plates (keyboard)
- arpeggiator
- note sequencer
UT envelope generator hard reset (off-on)


Pressure slew limiter
Creates slew limiting effect on pressure, slows down the
changes of outgoing voltage.
LT rise speed (0 to 99)
RT fall speed (0 to 99)

             - 18 -
Pressure recorder settings
LT recorder mode:
- SE (sequencer) records pressure data in sample'n'hold-
ish fashion, stores in a (maximum 32 step long) sequence
- TA (tape) records pressure data continuously, stores on a
5,5 second long virtual tape
RT recorder playmode (looping or one-shot)
BT reset source, mandatory when playmode is set to
one-shot (no source, keyboard, arpeggiator, note
sequencer)

Sequence length
LT length of recorder sequence (2 to 32)




Sequence clock
LT individual clock rate of sequencer, which can be the
division or multiplication of the internal clock/external sync
(:32, :24, :16, :12, :8, :6, :4, :3, :2, *1, *2,
*3, *4, *6, *8)

PORTAMENTO
SETTINGS
             Portamento time
             Creates slew limiting effect on the CV output, sets the speed
             of gliding (ie: how much time it takes to transition from one
             pitch to the next). The glide time will stay the same between
             notes, regardless of the interval.
             LT time (0 to 31)

             Portamento shape
             LT shape:
             - exponential
             - linear
             - logarithmic

             Portamento mode
             LT mode:
             - continuous
             - legato (gliding happens only when two or more note
             plates are touched simultaneously)

           BT vernier of modulation amount (0 to 3)
           RT pressure control over modulation depth (off-on)


           Vibrato speed, shape
           LT speed of LFO (0 to 63)
           BT vernier of speed (0 to 3)
           RT shape (triangle, square, smooth random, stepped
           random, positive offset, negative offset)

GATE AND TRIGGER OUTPUT SETTINGS




              - 23 -
 "Gate" output source
 LT source:
 - automatic
 - keyboard gate
 - keyboard trigger (great for exciting filters)
 - arp gate (when arp is disabled the module will output gate from keyboard)
 - note sequencer gate (when note sequencer is disabled the module will
 output gate from keyboard)
 - pressure (turns on when pressure reaches half its range)
 - random gate (running at 2x the speed of internal clock)
 - clock (internal clock multiplied by 4)
 RT NOT off-on (inverts output)
 BT AND off-on (output will present only when note plates are touched)

"Trigger" output source
Same as above, except:
- when arp gate is selected and arp is disabled the output will be muted
- when note sequencer gate is selected and the sequencer is disabled the
output will be muted
- clock outputs internal clock multiplied by 2

             - 29 -
                               SENSITIVITY, DEBOUNCE
                                & ENCODER DIRECTION


To adjust these attributes press and hold the encoder during power-on, rotate it to scroll through parameters.
Click the encoder to enable/disable editing of values. After setting these values the module needs to be
rebooted by turning off then on your system.

Some MPR121 breakout boards may behave differently, regarding the sensitivity of touch plates. If your
module in keyboard mode (with diasbled arpeggiator and sequencer) behaves like a totally unpredictable
sequencer clocked by a Geiger counter try to lower SENSITIVITY and/or increase DEBOUNCE. Try to
keep SENSITIVITY as high as possible and DEBOUNCE as low as possible.

The third parameter determines the direction of encoder rotation.

The fourth parameters enables/disables the internal voltage refernce of the DAC (digital-to-analog
converter). Set VR to IN if using DAC8562 or DAC8563, set VR to EX if using AD5663.
