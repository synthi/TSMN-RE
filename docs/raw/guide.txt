CLARIFICATION
                                                                                       #06   #07   #08   #09   #10
ARPEGGIATOR
ARPEGGIATOR WILL STEP THROUGH TOUCHED PLATES IN ORDER OF THEIR NUMBER (AS
SHOWN IN THE FIGURE). THIS MEANS THAT ARPEGGIATION WON’T NECESSARY HAPPEN IN
THE CLASSIC WAY, SINCE NOTE PLATES CAN BE CONFIGURED TO ANY NOTE. AFTER EXITING
SETTINGS PRESSING ENCODER SWITCH WILL ACTIVATE ‘HOLD’ FUNCTION.

ARPEGGIATOR VARIATOR                                                                   #01   #02   #03   #04   #05
ADDS ADDITIONAL NOTES TO ARPEGGIO. IN THE EXAMPLE: TSNM ADDS TWO MORE NOTES TO
ARPEGGIATED SEQUENCE, ADDITIONAL NOTES WILL BE 5 SEMITONES HIGHER COMPARED TO
THE PREVIOUS.

‘x2’ ARPEGGIO WITHOUT VARIATOR:
C1 - E1 - G1 ...
‘!2’ ARPEGGIO WITH VARIATOR:
C1 - F1 - A#1 - E1 - A1 - D2 - G1 - C2 - F2 ...
‘>2’ WITH VARIATIONS AT THE END OF ARPEGGIATED SEQUENCE:
C1 - E1 - G1 - F1 - A1 - C2 - A#1 - D2 - F2 ...

SEQUENCER
PLAYS A SEQUENCE OF PROGRAMMED NOTES WHICH IS TRANSPOSED BY NOTE PLATES. THE
RESULT CAN BE QUANTISED. AFTER EXITING SETTINGS PRESSING ENCODER SWITCH WILL
RESET THE SEQUENCE TO THE FIRST STEP.
