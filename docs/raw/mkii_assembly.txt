#33 / MISC. COMPONENTS

1x 15-PIN FEMALE HEADER
(J7 ON THE TOP SIDE OF PCB)
1x 15-PIN SMD MALE HEADER
(ON THE BACK OF THE FRONTPANEL)
- SLIDE THE FRONT PANEL ONTO THE BUSHBUTTONS, TIGHTEN THE SCREWS INTO THE SPACERS
- MAKE SURE THE SMD HEADER IS STANDING IN IT’S PLACE ON THE BACKSIDE OF THE FRONT PANEL
- START BY SOLDERING THE FEMALE HEADER TO THE PCB
- SOLDER THOSE PINS OF THE SMT HEADER THAT ARE VISIBLE
- REMOVE THE FRONT PANEL, PAY ATTENTION TO THE 15 PIN SMD MALE HEADER, PULL IT APART
GENTLY, NOT TO RIP OFF THE PADS AND TRACES FROM THE FRONT PANEL
- SOLDER THE REMAINING PINS OF THE SMD HEADER
#34 / MISC. COMPONENTS



POWER CONNECTOR
WIRE UP THE EDAC CONNECTOR TO THE PCB ACCORDING TO THE ILLUSTRATION. USE CCA. 35 CM LONG,
22AWG STRANDED WIRES. INSTALL A POLARIZING KEY (OR SOLDER A BLOB OF SOLDER) TO PIN 7.
FASTEN THE CABLES TO THE PCB WITH A CABLE TIE.
#35 / MISC. COMPONENTS

2x LED DISPLAY MATRIX1, MATRIX2
1x ENCODER ENC1
- INSERT THE MATRICES AND THE ENCODER, DON’T SOLDER THEM YET
- TO LINE UP THE ENCODER WITH THE SPACERS MOUNT AN EXTRA NUT OR WASHERS ON ITS THREAD
(THIS WILL NARROW THE GAP BETWEEN THE ENCODER AND THE PANEL)
- SLIDE THE FRONT PANEL ONTO ITS PLACE, TIGHTEN THE SCREWS INTO THE SPACERS
- TIGHTEN THE NUT ONTO THE ENCODERS THREAD
- ALIGN THE MATRICES THAT THEY LINE UP WITH THE HOLES ON THE FRONT PANEL AS ACCURATELY AS POSSIBLE
- NOW CAREFULLY SOLDER THE PREPARED COMPONENTS, RE-CHECK ALIGNMENT BEFORE MOVING TO ANOTHER PIN
- TIGHTEN THE NUTS
- PLACE THE ENCODER KNOB

ORIENTATION OF VARIOUS LED MATRICES:
- FYM-7571AUHR-11 TEXT SHOULD FACE ENCODER
- LTP-747KR TEXT SHOULD FACE JACKS
- TA07-11SURKWA TEXT SHOULD FACE ENCODER
- KWM-20571CSB TEXT SHOULD FACE ENCODER
